# Tutorial Agentic AI

A hands-on workspace for experimenting with Agentic AI, multimodal models, and practical AI-powered applications.

This repository contains learning projects and prototypes that explore how AI agents can understand text, images, videos, and user intent to support real-world decision-making.

## Prototypes

### 🩺 Health Report Analysis

An AI-powered health-report assistant that can extract values from blood reports, identify results that may be high or low based on the report's reference ranges, and generate simple diet-focused recommendations.

### 🛍️ Visual Shopping Assistant

A shopping assistant where users can upload a product image, identify the product using an AI vision model, compare listings across Indian e-commerce platforms, review prices and product details, and shortlist preferred products.

### 🎥 Retail Store Video Analytics

A computer-vision prototype that analyzes retail-store video footage to identify products selected or preferred by customers. The goal is to understand shopping behavior, popular products, and possible customer preferences from in-store interactions.

## Areas of Exploration

- Agentic AI workflows
- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- Vision and multimodal AI
- Image and video understanding
- Product search and recommendation
- E-commerce price comparison
- Retail analytics
- Health-report data extraction
- Streamlit application development
- API integrations with Groq, Gemini, and search services

## Goal

The goal of this repository is to learn by building practical AI prototypes, testing different models and APIs, and exploring how agentic systems can support users in healthcare, retail, and e-commerce use cases.
